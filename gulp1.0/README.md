## Part One: Core Concepts

### 1. Logic Link

A Logic Link is the **connection of causal relationships** in the evolutionary process, formed by the interaction between information, energy, structure, and goals.

It is **the basic unit of all systems**, capable of existing at different levels and dimensions:

- **Gene Chain**: Biological genetic information flow
- **Neural Network Chain**: Brain signal flow patterns
- **Civilization Knowledge Chain**: Society, culture, technology, and knowledge transmission
- **Transaction Chain**: Value exchange in economic and social activities
- **AI Weight Chain**: Parameter optimization and learning processes in machine learning models

### 2. Emergence Degree

Emergence Degree quantifies the evolutionary capacity of Logic Links, measuring their self-organization, adaptation, and innovation capabilities:

**Emergence Degree = Hierarchical Dimension × Intelligence Degree × Zero-Bug Degree × Unit Scale × Coefficient**

- **Hierarchical Dimension**: The organizational complexity of the system
- **Intelligence Degree**: The system's ability to select optimal paths and make decisions
- **Zero-Bug Degree**: Internal consistency and self-coherence
- **Unit Scale**: The smallest units participating in emergence

Zero-Bug Degree is the foundation for the stable evolution of Logic Links, ensuring the system's self-evolution and self-organization capacity.

## Part Four: Multi-Galactic Evolutionary Framework

### 1. Concept of Galactic Evolution

Galactic evolution refers to the process by which different hierarchical systems (universe, life, biology, civilization, capital, intelligence systems) undergo emergence and self-organization through Logic Links, forming cross-level, cross-dimensional evolutionary processes.

All galaxies follow unified laws:

**Logic Link Emergence → Life Cycle → Multi-Galactic Interaction**

### 2. Evolutionary Life Cycle

Each galaxy possesses a universal life cycle:

- **Initial Formation Period**: Logic Links begin to emerge, structures are preliminarily stable
- **Expansion Period**: Number of Logic Links increases, Hierarchical Dimension rises, system rapidly optimizes
- **Maturity Period**: Structure and function are highly stable, Emergence capacity reaches its maximum
- **Decline and Restructuring Period**: Changes in resources or environment lead to restructuring or decline of Logic Links, possibly re-entering an expansion period

### 3. Evolutionary Dynamics and Constraint Mechanisms

- **Logic Link Emergence**: Self-replication and optimization drive evolution
- **Ecological Space Constraints**: Determine the stability and evolutionary path of Logic Links

### 4. Multi-Galactic Interaction

Different galaxies interact through Logic Links, forming cross-dimensional evolution:

- Cosmic Galaxy Evolution: Physical gravity and matter exchange drive evolution
- Genetic Galaxy Evolution: Interaction between genes and ecosystems drives biological evolution
- Civilization Galaxy Evolution: Interaction between society, culture, economy, and technology drives civilizational progress
- Capital Galaxy Evolution: Capital, transactions, and intelligent decision-making drive economic system evolution
- Intelligence Galaxy Evolution: Trans-universal intelligence systems drive Logic Link evolution
